Noun classes: talking about people
Kinyarwanda nouns live in families. Learn the umu-/aba- family and plurals stop being guesswork.
Every Kinyarwanda noun belongs to a class, and the class is carried by a prefix. The people class pairs 
umu-
 (one person) with 
aba-
 (several). Swap the prefix, keep the stem — the rest of the sentence agrees with it.
